*Chien ailé* is an electroacoustic piece built with sound materials gathered in Nepal, in february 2019. 

Premiered in 2019, the piece was awarded with [Prix Pierre Schaeffer](https://phonurgia.fr/contestprogram2020/), Phonurgia Nova Awards 2020. 

> The winged dog wanders between the merciless aisles of dust, ochre lights, and indistinct scents. Roaming the labyrinth of a civilization that ignores him, he is a stranger everywhere, a perfect ethnologist. He moves on, without a destiny, offering on occasion his fleeting companionship to whoever knows how to cradle, in the hollow of a hand, the damp paw of a celestial friend.
