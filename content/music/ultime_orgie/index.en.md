*Ultime orgie* is an electroacoustic piece with video composed in 2020 on the occasion of the Ars Nova festival at the CNSMD in Lyon.

The piece was presented to the public during the [glitch art exhibition](https://fubar.space/2022/glitch-art-exhibition/) of the Fubar festival in Zagreb in 2022.

Music was created using sequences built with a DIY soundtracker, allowing me to write accurate score statements to the synthesis engine of *Csound*. Video uses DIY ASCII art processes.  

> *Ultime orgie* is conceived as a threshold on the verge of being crossed, a musical and video painting of that particular moment when humanity relinquishes utopia and everything that once brought it together.
