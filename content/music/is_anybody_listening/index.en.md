*Is anybody listening* ? is a composition for 3 children's electronic orchestras.

This work was commissionned byt the École Nationale de Musique de Villeurbanne, and created with the artistic support of GRAME.

Premiered on March 31, 2023, during the festival [Explorations numériques](https://www.enm-villeurbanne.fr/saison-publique/elektromomes-is-anybody-listening/) at ENM, this piece serves a dual artistic and pedagogical purpose.

> Is anybody listening ? is a sincere question, fueled by the bitterness felt when facing the absurdity of an old world that is slow to fade away. How can I not ask this question, when faced with the immense responsibility of having to create a work that must be championed by the representatives of a very young generation? How can I not be worried, and yet at the same time convinced that they will have to confront this painful question?
