Within the framework of the *Transgraphism* system designed by visual artist David Bartholoméo, we rediscover the snapshot, at the bend of a liminal performance where musical and pictorial gestures collide. A rediscovery of writing, drawn from a recovered unconscious, awakened in exhaustion after having broken, one by one, the artistic reflexes that define us and, in a certain way, imprison us.
