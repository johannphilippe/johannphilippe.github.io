Associated with GRAME, the IAC offers a collaborative performance in space, on the occasion of the closing of the exhibition *The drift* by sound artist and visual artist Tarek Atoui.

The [performance](https://www.grame.fr/productions/performance-tarek-atoui) invites four artists—Yui Sakagoshi, Jérôme Bertholon, Johann Philippe, and Nolwenn Guéhenneux—to improvise with Tarek and Jad Atoui, in a dialogue between his works and the space.
